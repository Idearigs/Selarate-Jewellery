The Atelier

One room, one bench, one pair of hands.

Everything sold here is made in a single room: drawn, alloyed, forged, set and finished without leaving the building. No casting house, no outside setter, no subcontracted polish.

That limit is the reason the collection is small. It is also the reason a piece can be repaired, resized or reworked twenty years from now by the person who made it.
