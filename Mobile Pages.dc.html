PORTRAIT — 4:5
The Maker

Twenty-five years at the same bench.

Gemologist and goldsmith. Every stone is chosen by hand, every setting cut, set and finished in the studio. Nothing leaves the bench until it can be worn every day for fifty years.

A practice built on one rule: make it once, make it right.

The studio began with a bench, a torch and a drawer of rough stone. It has stayed roughly that size on purpose. Work is taken in small numbers so that each piece can be drawn around the stone in front of it.

Stones are bought loose, often years before they are set. Gold is alloyed and rolled in house, which is why the colour shifts a little between pieces.

BENCH — 4:3
The Atelier

One room, one bench, one pair of hands.

Everything sold here is made in a single room: drawn, alloyed, forged, set and finished without leaving the building. No casting house, no outside setter.

That limit is why the collection is small. It is also why a piece can be repaired twenty years from now by the person who made it.
